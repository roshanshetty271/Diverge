## Startup and Business Statistics

### Failure Rates
- 90% of startups fail overall (Digital Silk 2026 analysis, CB Insights data)
- Failure by timeline: 20% fail in year 1, 50% by year 5, 65% by year 10 (Bureau of Labor Statistics 2025)
- AI startups specifically: 90% failure rate with 18-month median lifespan before pivot or shutdown (TechCrunch/Crunchbase 2025 analysis)

### Why Startups Fail
- 42% fail from lack of product-market fit — the product doesn't solve a real problem (CB Insights 2025 post-mortem analysis, n=472)
- 29% run out of cash (but this is usually a symptom of #1)
- 19% get outcompeted
- 16% have pricing/cost model issues
- Only 8% cite team problems as primary cause (though team issues compound other failures)

### Founder Success Rates
- First-time founder success rate: 18% (Startup Genome Report 2025)
- Second-time founders (after a failure): 20% — the "experience premium" is smaller than people think
- Founders with a co-founder succeed 2.7x more often than solo founders (Startup Genome 2025)
- 74% of successful founders had deep domain expertise in their startup's industry (First Round Capital 2025 review)

### Financial Reality
- Median founder salary in year 1-2: $0-$50K depending on funding (Kruze Consulting 2025)
- Average time to profitability for startups that survive: 2-3 years (SBA 2025)
- Median startup runway at founding: 18 months with seed funding, 6 months bootstrapped (Carta 2025)

### What This Means For Decision-Making
The 90% failure rate is real but misleading in isolation. The #1 reason startups die is building something nobody wants, not running out of money or working too little. Founders who validate demand before quitting their job, maintain 12+ months of personal runway, and have domain expertise dramatically improve their odds. The financial sacrifice is real: expect 1-3 years of significantly reduced income.
